Why missed LSA calls cost more than one job

Every Google Local Services Ads call is a lead you paid for. When nobody picks up, you lose that customer, and you also hurt the responsiveness signal Google uses to rank LSA advertisers. Google says plainly that missed calls can hurt responsiveness.

Contractors miss calls for normal reasons: the crew is on a roof, the office is on another line, or it’s 9 PM on a Sunday. A generic answering service or voicemail doesn’t fix the ranking problem. It also doesn’t give you what you need to call the customer back quickly.

The stakes are going up. As LSA moves into Google Ads, Google says a missed call during business hours can be charged as a lead if the caller stays on the line for more than 20 seconds. Read our guide to LSA’s move into Performance Max for details.

How LSA Command answers every call: humans first, AI always

  1. First, the team gets its shot. Every LSA call rings the business's phones first, for about 10 seconds. If someone answers, it's a normal call and the AI stays out of the way.
  2. No pickup: the call transfers and AI answers instantly. If nobody answers (busy, on a job, after hours), the LSA Command AI picks up on the spot. It qualifies the caller, captures the job details, and books the job or warm-transfers a hot lead while the caller is still on the line.
  3. Either way, the call is answered within 30 seconds. Google can charge for a missed call during business hours when the caller stays on the line more than 20 seconds. An answered call is a conversation with a customer, not a missed call.
  4. Then the notes and notifications go out. Lead notes are written, and a text and an email go to the client, your agency, or both. Google's own notification for a phone lead is just an email with no recording or transcript.

Robocalls never reach your phone

Paid LSA numbers attract spam. LSA Command runs a three-layer defense that stops robocalls before your phone rings and counts every block in your dashboard. Your team spends its time on real customers.

Open 24 hours, without a night shift

Being “open now” when competitors are closed earns you placement at nights, on weekends and on holidays. Those are often the searches where homeowners have an urgent problem and will book whoever answers.

Frequently asked questions

Will the AI answer calls my team could have taken?

No. Every call rings the team first for about 10 seconds. The call transfers to the AI only when no one picks up.

Does answering every call improve my Google LSA ranking?

Responsiveness is one of the factors Google uses to rank Local Services Ads, and Google says missed calls can hurt it. Answering every call within 30 seconds protects that signal. Ranking also depends on proximity, reviews, and business hours, so no tool can promise a specific position.

What do I receive after the AI handles a call?

Lead notes with an AI summary, the full transcript and the call recording, plus a text and an email notification with the caller’s number, so the callback starts with what the customer needs. High-value leads identified.

Can the AI transfer a hot lead to me?

Yes. Qualified callers can be warm-transferred straight to your phone while they’re still on the line.
