What is included for every client

Want to see it before you read about it? See exactly what you get: the texts, emails and screens, shown with sample data.

Notes on every lead, added to Performance Max

LSA Command writes notes on every lead and adds them to Performance Max. Each lead gets an AI summary, the transcript and the recording, and text and email notifications go to the client, the agency or both. High-value leads identified.

AI call answering

The client's team gets 10 to 15 seconds to pick up. If they do not, the call transfers and the AI answers instantly, so calls are answered within 30 seconds.

AI messaging

Instant replies to LSA message leads, with each reply verified through the LSA API.

Lead ratings and credit tracking

Every lead rated back to Google as lead feedback, spam and invalid leads flagged for credit, and credits tracked.

Google survey filled out

LSA Command fills out Google's 1 to 5 lead survey after every lead, so the client's team doesn't have to.

Review generation

AI follow-up after the job with the client's review link, and replies to reviews.

Robocall screening and call grading

Spam is kept off the client's phone and out of the lead count. The AI agent and the client's intake team are scored on every call.

Agency view and white-label

All your client accounts in one place, a dashboard for each client, your brand on it, and GoHighLevel integration.

Pricing questions

How much does LSA Command cost?

LSA Command costs $200 per month per client. Every feature is included.

Is pricing per client or per agency?

Per client. You pay $200 per month for each client account you run on LSA Command. Five clients is $1,000 per month and ten clients is $2,000 per month.

Is there a setup fee?

No. There is no setup fee.

What is included in the price?

Every feature: AI call answering, AI messaging for message leads, lead notes on every lead, added to Performance Max, text and email notifications, High-value leads identified, Google's 1 to 5 lead survey filled out, lead ratings back to Google, lead credit tracking, robocall screening, review generation, call grading, GoHighLevel integration and white-label options.

What is the LSA account audit?

It is what we do on your walkthrough. You bring one client's Google LSA account to a 30-minute walkthrough with our team, and we pull its response rate, lead quality and lead volume live.

Are lead notes added to Performance Max?

Yes. LSA Command writes notes on every lead and adds them to Performance Max, for accounts on Google's Performance Max version of LSA. It is included in the price.

Can we start with one client?

Yes. Start with one client, see it working, and then roll it out to the rest of your book.

Does creating an agency account turn anything on?

No. Signing up does not activate phone numbers or live client services. Nothing goes live for a client until setup and billing have been confirmed with you.

Does the price include what Google charges for leads?

No. The price covers the LSA Command software. Google's charges for Local Services Ads leads are separate and stay between the advertiser and Google.

What should we charge our clients?

That is your call. You set your own service offering and your own client price. Our guide to pricing LSA management for clients walks through the common models.

Can a business owner buy LSA Command directly?

No. LSA Command is sold only to marketing agencies. If you own a business, ask your marketing agency about LSA Command.
