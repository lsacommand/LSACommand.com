What the Local Services Ads lead inbox is

The lead inbox is where all of your Google LSA leads land. Google's help page on managing your leads says that from your lead inbox you can review booked jobs, listen to phone lead recordings (US only), respond to leads, and review phone calls generated by your ad.

Open any lead and you get a panel called the Lead Summary. It holds the fields that describe the lead: Lead Status, Customer Name, Location, Additional Details and a few more.

Here is the catch. A phone call does not mark itself as booked. The Booked status, the job and the appointment only get into the Lead Summary if somebody goes in and adds them. On a busy account, that step is easy to skip.

So the Google LSA lead inbox fills up with calls that all look alike. A booked job and a wrong number sit side by side, and neither one is marked Booked.

How to mark LSA leads as booked yourself in Google's lead inbox

You can do all of this by hand. Google's help page on Local Services Ads reports gives the steps:

  1. Open the Local Services Ads inbox.
  2. Choose a lead.
  3. Select Mark Booked.
  4. Select the date, or indicate that the job is completed.
  5. Add details for tracking.
  6. Save.

The same page explains why it is worth doing. Google says Local Services ad reports help you "Measure if those leads convert to bookings." When you mark a lead as booked, you can add details like job type and customer name to track your leads, and you can use the filters on the Reports page to find those leads later.

Two notes from Google: these details are only visible to you, and marking leads as booked is currently available only in the U.S. and Canada.

Six steps is not hard. Six steps on every booked call, on every client account, every week, is where it falls apart. Somebody has to listen to the call, find the lead, and type the details. It is the step that gets skipped.
