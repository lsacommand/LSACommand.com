The LSA Command Team

An agency team with almost two decades of experience, building software so every Google Local Services Ads lead gets answered.

Why we built LSA Command

LSA Command was built by an agency team that has spent almost two decades working with local businesses. We talked with business owners every day, and we kept hearing the same thing: the leads were coming in, but the jobs weren't.

So we looked at the numbers. We opened our clients' Local Services Ads dashboards and compared total calls against connected calls. The answer was black and white. 20 to 30 percent of calls were being missed.

That was hard to see. Our agency was sending great leads through Local Services Ads, and those leads were going unanswered or never getting a follow-up. The ads were doing their job. The missed calls were the problem.

We built the fix for our own agency first. It started as an AI voice and message response tool called DIY AI, made so a business could set up its own voice agent to answer calls and reply to message leads.

Then we saw there was more to do to help businesses and agencies convert their LSA leads. Answering was only the start. Booked calls needed to be marked as Booked in Google's Lead Summary, teams needed notifications, and robocalls needed to be blocked. DIY AI grew into LSA Command.

Everything we write here comes from that same work: helping businesses and agencies answer every lead and turn more of them into booked jobs.

What LSA Command does today

LSA Command makes sure 100% of your Local Services Ads calls are answered within 30 seconds. Your team gets the first 10 to 15 seconds to pick up. If they can't, the AI answers. Message leads get an instant AI reply.

  • Marks booked calls as Booked in Google's Lead Summary, inside Google's LSA platform, with the customer name, location and appointment in Additional Details
  • Sends notifications to your team
  • Blocks robocalls
  • Costs $200 per month per client
