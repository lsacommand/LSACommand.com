Why pool contractors lose Google LSA leads

Pool leads cover very different jobs: weekly cleaning, green-pool cleanups, equipment repair, and new pool construction worth tens of thousands of dollars. Answering each one quickly, and telling Google which jobs you actually want, decides whether your LSA budget fills the schedule with builds or with one-off cleanings.

Pool demand is also seasonal. The spring opening rush hits every pool company in your area at once.

What LSA Command does for pool contractors

LSA Command handles the LSA leads for pool construction, pool cleaning and maintenance, green-pool cleanups, equipment and pump repair, and resurfacing. You get the same system on every lead:

  • AI call answering: your team rings first, and if nobody picks up in 10 to 15 seconds the call transfers and AI answers instantly, so every call is answered within 30 seconds.
  • Notes and notifications: notes on every lead, added to Performance Max, plus text and email notifications to the business, the agency, or both.
  • Message lead response: replies instantly, verified through the LSA API so none silently drop.
  • Lead feedback and credits: every lead scored and rated back to Google; spam and invalid leads flagged for credit.
  • Review generation: AI follow-up after every job and a reply to every review.

What you get on every lead

Every call and message gets lead notes: an AI summary, the transcript and the recording, plus a Google LSA rating and an AI performance score. A text and an email notification go to the business, the agency, or both. High-value leads identified, so the new build or the leak emergency gets the first callback. Google’s own notification for a phone lead is just an email.
