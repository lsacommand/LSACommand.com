Why electricians lose Google LSA leads

Electrical LSA leads range from “half my house has no power” to panel upgrades and EV charger installs. Many homeowners send a message instead of calling, especially for planned work. If those replies are slow, or silently fail to reach them, they move on to the next listing.

Electricians also work in attics, crawlspaces, and on commercial jobs where answering the phone isn’t realistic. That’s where responsiveness slips.

What LSA Command does for electricians

LSA Command handles the LSA leads for power outages and troubleshooting, panel upgrades, EV charger installation, lighting, and wiring repairs. You get the same system on every lead:

  • AI call answering: your team rings first, and if nobody picks up in 10 to 15 seconds the call transfers and AI answers instantly, so every call is answered within 30 seconds.
  • Notes and notifications: notes on every lead, added to Performance Max, plus text and email notifications to the business, the agency, or both.
  • Message lead response: replies instantly, verified through the LSA API so none silently drop.
  • Lead feedback and credits: every lead scored and rated back to Google; spam and invalid leads flagged for credit.
  • Review generation: AI follow-up after every job and a reply to every review.

What you get on every lead

Every call and message gets lead notes: an AI summary, the transcript and the recording, plus a Google LSA rating and an AI performance score. A text and an email notification go to the business, the agency, or both. High-value leads identified, so the panel upgrade and the urgent outage get the first callbacks. Google’s own notification for a phone lead is just an email.

Frequently asked questions

Are Google Local Services Ads worth it for electricians?

LSA puts electricians at the very top of Google with the Google Verified badge, and you pay per lead instead of per click. It pays off when you answer every lead fast, rate leads back to Google, and keep your service area tight. LSA Command handles the first two.

Do message leads matter for electricians on LSA?

Yes, especially for planned projects like panel upgrades and EV chargers, where homeowners often message instead of calling. LSA Command replies instantly and checks through the LSA API that each reply actually reached the customer.
