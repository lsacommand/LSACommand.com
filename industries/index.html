The same system on every account

  • AI call answering: the client's team gets about 10 seconds to pick up, then the call transfers and AI answers instantly, so calls are answered within 30 seconds.
  • Message lead response: instant AI replies, verified through the LSA API.
  • Notes and notifications: notes on every lead, added to Performance Max, plus text and email notifications. High-value leads identified.
  • Lead ratings and credits: every lead rated back to Google, spam and invalid leads flagged for credit.
  • Review generation: AI follow-up after the job and a reply to reviews.

Results so far

Our published case studies come from three pool contractors, with names kept private. Across the three accounts, leads answered in under 30 seconds went from 28% to 100% and combined monthly leads grew from 38 to 147.
