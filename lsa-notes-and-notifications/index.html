A paid lead is only worth something if the right person hears about it and knows what to do next. That is the job of notes and notifications: every lead written up, scored and put in front of the people who need to act on it.

Text and email notifications for every lead

The gap in Google's own notification

Since Google retired the Local Services Ads mobile app in January 2025, its notification for a phone lead is an email. It says a potential customer called. It has no text message, no call recording, no summary and no transcript, and the business has to log in to learn more.

Emails get missed. In the LSA accounts we audit, 10 to 15% of paid leads routinely show no owner response at all, usually because the only notification was an email nobody saw. For an agency, that is a client paying for leads and blaming the ads. Our guide to Google LSA lead notifications covers what Google sends and how to set it up properly.

What is in the lead notes

  • AI summary. A short read on who called, what they need and what was agreed on the call.
  • Transcript. The full conversation, word for word.
  • Recording. One tap to hear the call as it happened.
  • Google LSA rating. The lead's rating, which is also sent back to Google as lead feedback. See lead disputes and credits.
  • AI performance score. How well the AI handled the call. When the client's own team handles a lead, their intake is scored too.
  • Google's lead survey, filled out. LSA Command fills out Google's 1 to 5 lead survey after every lead, so the client's team doesn't have to.

Notes are also written into the lead in the client's Google LSA dashboard, so it stops being a list of "Potential customer" entries.

High-value leads identified

Not every lead deserves the same urgency. LSA Command labels the best leads, and the label follows the lead: the lead notes, the lead list and the summary at the top of the client dashboard.

For the client, that means the owner or office manager knows which callback to make first. For your agency, it is the simplest way to show a client that the leads they paid for were worth having.

Text and email notifications

  • Both channels. A text and an email for each lead, sent as the lead is handled.
  • To the right people. The client, the agency, or both.
  • Enough to act on. The text gives the lead type, the customer's phone number and a link to the conversation. The email adds the customer's name, the location and the transcript.
  • A way in. The link also lets a person take over the conversation themselves.
  • No noise from spam. Calls flagged as robocalls are logged and do not send a notification.

Notes and notifications are the last two steps of one flow. Calls ring the client's team first for about 10 seconds. If nobody picks up, the call transfers and the AI answers instantly, so calls are answered within 30 seconds. Message leads get an instant AI reply. Either way, the notes and the notification follow.

Frequently asked questions

What is in the lead notes?

Every lead gets an AI summary, the full transcript and the call recording, plus a Google LSA rating and an AI performance score.

Are the notes added to Performance Max?

Yes. LSA Command writes notes on every lead and adds them to Performance Max, for accounts on Google's Performance Max version of LSA.

Who receives the notifications?

Text and email notifications go to the client, the agency, or both. You decide who is on the list for each client.

What does High-value leads identified mean?

It is the label LSA Command puts on the best leads. It appears in the lead notes, in the lead list and in the dashboard summary, so the client's team knows which leads to call back first.

Are notifications sent for message leads as well as calls?

Yes. Calls and message leads both get lead notes and a text and email notification.

Do robocalls trigger a notification?

No. Calls flagged as robocalls are logged in the dashboard and do not send a notification.

Does this replace Google's own lead email?

No. Google still sends its own email for a phone lead. LSA Command adds a text, a fuller email and lead notes, so the client does not have to log in to find out what the caller wanted.

Can notifications carry our agency's brand?

Yes. LSA Command has white-label options, including sending email from a mailbox on your agency's domain. The setup guide shows how.
