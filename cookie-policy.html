Cookie Policy

How LSA Command uses cookies

This policy explains the cookies and similar tracking technologies that we use on our website and how you can manage them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help websites remember preferences, provide functionality, and analyze site performance.

Cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: necessary for basic site operation and navigation.
  • Analytics cookies: used to collect anonymous usage information such as page visits and load times.
  • Preference cookies: remember display settings and language preferences.

Why we use cookies

Cookies help us improve the website experience, understand how visitors use the site, and keep the site secure and stable.

Third-party cookies

We may allow third-party services, such as analytics providers, to set cookies on our site. These cookies are managed by the third party and subject to their privacy policies.

Managing cookies

You can manage cookies through your browser settings, including deleting cookies or blocking cookies entirely. Please note that disabling cookies may affect some website features and performance.

Cookie changes

We may update this policy as our use of cookies changes. Please review this page periodically for the latest information.
