What the $200 covers

There is one price and every feature is in it. That gives you one number for a client proposal: your software cost is $200 a month, and the client price beside it is yours to fill in.

Calls answered within 30 seconds, and instant AI messaging

100% of calls are answered within 30 seconds. The client's team rings first for 10 to 15 seconds. If they are on a job or on another line, the voice AI answers. It works 24/7, can transfer the caller to a person, and can book appointments on the calendar. Message leads get an AI reply instantly.

Speed matters for what your client gets for the money, too. Google's current help page on how leads work says a valid lead includes a missed phone call during your business hours when the customer stays on the line for more than 20 seconds. So your client can pay for a call nobody picked up. Answering every call means each lead they pay for gets a conversation. More on AI call answering and how Google's phone response score works.

Notes on every lead, and notifications under your brand

Every lead gets notes: an AI summary, the transcript and the recording. The notes are also written onto the lead in Google LSA, so the record there carries the same context. Instant text and email notifications go to the client, the agency, or both, under your agency's brand. Details are on the notes and notifications page.

Booked calls marked inside Google's LSA platform

When the AI books an appointment on a Google LSA call, LSA Command fills out the Lead Summary directly inside Google's own LSA platform. It sets Lead Status to Booked, fills in Customer Name and Location, and writes the job and the appointment into Additional Details. No copy-pasting, and it is part of the same $200.

High-value leads identified, and the weekly lead summary

You get High-value leads identified as they come in, so the owner knows which callbacks matter most. Once a week, a lead summary rates each lead as high or low value. That is the page you bring to a client check-in when they ask what the ads produced.

The busywork: survey, lead feedback, robocalls blocked

Google's 1 to 5 lead survey is filled out on every lead, and every lead is rated back to Google as lead feedback. Google says the Lead Feedback Survey helps it send better-suited leads.

Google decides every credit. LSA Command flags leads that look like spam, robocalls, wrong numbers or invalid leads, and tracks three separate columns: our assessment, feedback submitted, and credit recorded by Google. A credit only counts once Google records it. Google's help page says charged leads are reassessed by its models over time and may be credited automatically. Google says credits are not available for health care verticals, tax specialists, or advertisers in EMEA. See lead disputes and credits.

Robocalls are blocked before the client's phone rings. Each block is logged and kept out of the lead count. Review generation and replies are included, and so is call grading for the AI and for the client's intake team.

White label and the agency workspace

Your clients see your agency's logo and URL, not ours. You work from one agency workspace that holds your whole client book. To see the screens, go to what you get. For how it fits an agency's service, see the agencies page.

The math for your client book

The price is the same for every client, so the math is multiplication:

  • 1 client: $200 per month
  • 5 clients: $1,000 per month
  • 10 clients: $2,000 per month

You set your own client price and you keep the client relationship. Here is an example only, not a claim about typical agency prices: if an agency charges a client $500 a month for LSA management, the software line is $200 of that, and $300 is left to cover the agency's time and margin. Published results: what agencies' clients saw

Across three pool contractors, leads answered within 30 seconds rose from 28% to 100% between December 2025 and April 2026 (April is month to date through April 23, average of the three accounts). Combined monthly LSA leads went from 38 in December 2025 to 147 in March 2026 (3.9x), and average lead quality score rose from 2.43 to 3.87. Pool demand is seasonal and December is slow. Measured from November, combined leads still grew from 53 to 147 (2.8x).

One account from that group of results, in detail: a Bay Area pool contractor went from answering 18% of its Local Services Ads leads within 30 seconds in December 2025 to answering 100% by April 23, 2026. Monthly leads went from 17 in December to 56 in March, a 3.3x increase, and lead quality rose from 2.35 to 4.13. December is a slow month for pool work, so read the volume change alongside response rate and lead quality. More in the case studies.

What LSA Command pricing does not include

The $200 is for the software. It does not include Google's lead charges. Those are separate, and Google bills them to the advertiser.

Per Google's How leads work page, the advertiser pays for valid leads, and follow-ups from the same customer within 15 days are not charged again. Each lead carries a charge status: Charged, Not charged, In review, or Credited. For what those charges look like, see what Google Local Services Ads cost.

Frequently asked questions

LSA Command costs $200 per month per client. Every feature and voice AI are included, and there is no setup fee. Google's lead charges are separate and billed by Google.

Per client. Each client you add is $200 per month, so 5 clients is $1,000 a month and 10 clients is $2,000 a month.

Every feature is included in the $200 per month per client: voice AI, notes on every lead, notifications under your agency's brand, High-value leads identified, the weekly lead summary, the Google lead survey, robocall blocking and white label. There is no setup fee.
Are there any setup or onboarding fees?
No. There is no setup fee, and setup usually takes one call.

No. Google's lead charges are separate. Google bills them to the advertiser, and the $200 covers the LSA Command software only.
