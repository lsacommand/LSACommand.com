How to Get Google LSA Lead Notifications (Now That the App Is Gone)

Google retired the Local Services Ads mobile app in January 2025. For a phone lead, its only notification now is an email. Here’s why that’s a problem and how to fix it.

By the LSA Command Team · March 26, 2026

On this page

  1. What Google sends you today
  2. What a useful lead notification includes
  3. Faster callbacks, better ranking
  4. Frequently asked questions

What Google sends you today

Since the LSA app was retired in January 2025, Google’s notification for a phone lead is an email. There’s no text message, no call recording, no summary, and no transcript. To see details, you log into the dashboard.

Email is easy to miss. A contractor on a job site isn’t watching email, and a lead nobody sees is a lead nobody calls back. In the LSA accounts we audit, 10 to 15% of paid leads routinely show no owner response at all, usually because the only notification was an email nobody saw.

What a useful lead notification includes

  • A text message, as each lead is handled, to you and anyone on your team
  • The customer’s number, one tap to call back (including the extension Google assigns on message leads)
  • The call recording, so you hear exactly what was said
  • A short AI summary of what the customer wants, plus the full transcript
  • A takeover link to jump into a live message conversation yourself

That’s what LSA Command sends on every call and message lead, along with lead notes and a “High-value leads identified” label on the best ones. You call back in minutes, already knowing the job. See LSA Command notes and notifications.

Faster callbacks, better ranking

Speed matters twice in LSA: the first business to respond usually wins the job, and responsiveness is a ranking factor. Pair text and email notifications with AI call answering so leads are handled even when nobody can pick up.

Frequently asked questions

Is there still a Google Local Services Ads app?

Google retired the standalone LSA mobile app in January 2025. Phone-lead notifications now come by email.

Can I get LSA lead notifications by text message?

Not from Google directly for phone leads. LSA Command texts and emails you for every lead, with the caller’s number, recording, AI summary, and transcript.
