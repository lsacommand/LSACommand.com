What you get with LSA Command, screen by screen

LSA Command writes notes on every lead and adds them to Performance Max. These are the texts, emails and screens your client and your team see along the way. Every image uses sample data.

Book a 30-minute walkthrough

Your walkthrough includes an LSA account audit: bring one client's Google LSA account and we pull its response rate, lead quality and lead volume live. $200 per month per client.

Text and email notifications for every lead

As each lead is handled, a text and an email go to the client, your agency, or both. The text gives the lead type, the customer's phone number and a link to the conversation. The email adds the customer's name, the location and the transcript.

Take over the conversation

When a customer replies, the notification includes a link. One tap opens the conversation in the browser, where a person can read every message, type a reply, and choose whether the AI keeps replying.

Daily AI report and monthly summary emails

A report email links to the live lead report. A monthly summary email recaps the month: jobs grouped by type, lead volume, response rate and lead quality, compared with the month before.

Other notification emails

Two more emails LSA Command sends. One says a customer has replied and links to take over the conversation. The other says an AI call ended early and gives the number to call back.

Google LSA 1 to 5 lead survey, filled out for you

Google's lead survey asks for a rating from 1 to 5 and the reasons. LSA Command fills it out every time one comes up, so the client's team doesn't have to.

Notes added to Performance Max

LSA Command writes notes on every lead and adds them to Performance Max. This applies to accounts on Google's Performance Max version of LSA.

All of this is included for every client at $200 per month. AI messaging responds instantly to message leads, calls are answered within 30 seconds, and the notes and notifications follow each lead.

And the lead notes themselves

Every lead gets lead notes: an AI summary, the transcript and the recording, plus a Google LSA rating and an AI performance score. High-value leads identified. How notes and notifications work.
