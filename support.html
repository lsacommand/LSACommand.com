How can we help?

What we can help with

  • Your account. Agency account access, white-label branding, your web address and email settings. The setup guide walks through each step.
  • Google LSA connection. Connecting a client's Local Services Ads account and checking that leads are coming through.
  • Leads. Call answering, message replies, lead notes, and text and email notifications. See notes and notifications.
  • Billing. Questions about your LSA Command billing. See pricing.

Need Google itself? Our guide lists how to contact Google LSA support.

What to include

To help us investigate, tell us:

  • Your agency name, the client account involved, and the email address used for your LSA Command account.
  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead.
  • The error message, approximate time, and a screenshot if useful.

Please do not send passwords, API keys, payment-card details, or access tokens. Hide customer contact details in screenshots unless they are needed to explain the issue.

Account and privacy requests

Use the same number for questions about account access, billing, disconnecting an integration, or requesting access to or deletion of your data. We may need to verify account ownership before making changes.
