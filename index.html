The call flow, step by step

  4. The AI books the appointment, transfers to your team when the caller needs a person, and sends the lead with call notes.

Why agencies add LSA Command

Google made response speed your problem. Your clients' phones did not get easier to answer.

You can run the bids, the budget and the profile perfectly and still lose the account when leads go cold. Four things raise the stakes on every client you manage.

Missed calls can be billed

Google can charge a missed call during business hours as a lead if the caller stays on the line for more than 20 seconds. Voicemail does not protect the client. How missed-call charges work

Google's phone-lead notification is an email

Since Google retired the LSA mobile app in January 2025, a phone lead arrives as an email with no recording, summary or transcript. In the LSA accounts we audit, 10 to 15% of paid leads routinely show no owner response at all.

Responsiveness feeds ranking

Google says responsiveness to calls and messages is part of how Local Services Ads are ranked, and that missed calls can hurt it. How LSA ranking works

Credits are decided by Google

Most lead credits now run through Google's automated system, and Google no longer credits "job type not serviced" or "geo not serviced" leads. What you feed back to Google matters more than what you dispute. Lead disputes and credits

AI call answering

The client's team gets first shot. AI covers the rest.

Customers want a person, and Google wants the call answered. Every LSA call rings the client's team first. If nobody picks up in 10 to 15 seconds, because they are on a roof, on another line or closed for the night, the call transfers and the AI answers instantly.

  • No voicemail. The AI qualifies the caller, takes the job details and can book the job or warm-transfer a hot lead while the caller is still on the line.
  • Nights, weekends and holidays. With every call and message answered, the client can list the business as open 24 hours.
  • Robocalls screened out. Spam is stopped before the client's phone rings and kept out of the lead count.

How AI call answering works

One incoming LSA call

  1. Ring

    The call rings the client's team. People get priority for 10 to 15 seconds.

  2. Pickup

    Someone answers? It is a normal call and the AI stays out of the way.

  3. No pickup

    The call transfers and the AI answers instantly. The call is answered within 30 seconds.

  4. After

    Lead notes are written and notifications go out by text and email, with the recording and transcript.

Answering a call does not make it free. A real customer can still be a charged lead. The difference is that the customer talks to someone instead of calling the next company on the list.

Message leads

Every message lead gets an instant reply, and the reply is checked

AI messaging responds instantly to LSA message leads, day or night. Then LSA Command does the part most tools skip: it checks the lead thread through the LSA API to confirm the reply arrived.

  • "Delivered" is not always delivered. In the accounts we monitor, 10 to 15% of replies sent over SMS never land in the LSA thread. If a reply has not landed within minutes, LSA Command pushes it through the API.
  • Recovered messages are counted. Each one shows in the dashboard as an exact count.
  • Works when people reply too. If the client's team answers messages themselves, their replies are verified the same way.

Reviews

More Google reviews, asked for the right way

Rating and number of reviews count toward profile quality in the LSA auction. LSA Command follows up with customers by call or text after the job, asks how it went and sends the client's review link while the job is fresh.

  • Every customer gets the same request, in line with Google's review policy, and writes the review in their own words.
  • One polite reminder.
  • Unhappy customers also reach the owner, who gets a heads-up to call them personally.
  • Reviews get a reply under the client's business name.

Questions agencies ask before they start

What is LSA Command?

LSA Command is Google Local Services Ads software for marketing agencies. It writes notes on every lead and adds them to Performance Max. For every client it also answers LSA calls within 30 seconds, replies to message leads instantly, sends text and email notifications, rates every lead back to Google, flags bad leads for credit and asks for reviews. Your agency gets one view of every client, and each client gets a dashboard.

Does LSA Command work with accounts on Performance Max?

Yes. LSA Command writes notes on every lead and adds them to Performance Max, for accounts on Google's Performance Max version of LSA. Notes and notifications

What is the LSA account audit?

It is what we do on your walkthrough. You bring one client's Google LSA account to a 30-minute walkthrough with Ryan, and we pull its response rate, lead quality and lead volume live. You share your screen, so no passwords are needed.

Who can buy LSA Command?

Marketing agencies only. Agencies run it for their home-service clients, including HVAC companies, plumbers, roofers, electricians and pool contractors. We do not sell directly to businesses. If you own a business, ask your marketing agency about LSA Command.

How much does LSA Command cost?

$200 per month per client, with every feature included. Five clients is $1,000 per month and ten clients is $2,000 per month. We confirm setup and billing with your agency before any client goes live. See pricing

Will the AI answer calls my client's team could have taken?

No. Every call rings the client's team first for 10 to 15 seconds. Only if nobody picks up does the call transfer to the AI, which answers instantly. Either way the call is answered within 30 seconds. How call answering works

Can my agency run LSA Command under its own brand?

Yes. LSA Command has white-label options, so your agency stays out front with the client. You keep the relationship and set your own service offering. We provide the platform behind you. How agencies run it

Can we start with one client?

Yes. Start with one client, see it working, and then roll it out to the rest of your book. Nothing goes live for a client until setup and billing have been confirmed with you.

Who owns the client's Local Services Ads account?

That is between your agency and your client. LSA Command connects to an account with authorized access and does not change who owns it.

Does answering every call stop Google's missed-call charges?

Not exactly. Google's missed-call rule applies to calls nobody answers. An answered call is judged like any other lead, so a real customer can still be a charged lead. The difference is that the customer gets answered and qualified instead of calling the next company on the list. Missed-call charges explained

How do Google LSA lead credits work now?

Google handles most LSA lead disputes through an automated credit system and asks advertisers to rate leads with lead feedback. LSA Command scores every lead, rates it back to Google, flags spam and invalid leads for credit, and tracks the credits Google records. Lead disputes and credits

How do I help a client rank higher in Google Local Services Ads?

Google ranks Local Services Ads in an auction that weighs the bid, how likely the ad is to get a lead (including responsiveness to calls and messages), and profile quality: rating, number of reviews, response time, photos and verification checks. Responsiveness is usually the fastest to improve: answer every call and message quickly. How LSA ranking works

What happens to Local Services Ads with Performance Max?

Google is moving LSA into Google Ads as a Performance Max campaign type. Clients still pay per lead and still show on Search and Maps, but bidding, budgets and missed-call billing are changing. Read the Performance Max guide
