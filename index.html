Robo-Calls? They Never Reach You.

Blocked At The Carrier

Every new spam pattern the AI hears becomes a new blocking rule. A trick works once, then it's blocked for every client, forever. The filter gets stronger with every call across the whole network.

Ultimate Response Defense

100% of LSA Message Leads Answered

The dirty secret of LSA message leads: replies travel back to Google over SMS. Your phone (or any generic AI agent) sees "delivered." But in the accounts we monitor, 10–15% of the time the reply never lands in the LSA thread and never reaches the customer. It happens to human replies and off-the-shelf AI bots alike, and the only way to even see it is to log into LSA and open the lead, so nobody ever knows. LSA Command is built on the LSA API: it watches the actual lead thread after every reply, and if your response hasn't landed within minutes, it force-pushes it through the API for a true 100% response rate.

Zero Dropped Leads

"Delivered" doesn't mean delivered. Replies sent over SMS can fail to reach the LSA thread. Our watchdog checks the actual thread after every response. If it isn't there within minutes, the API hard-injects it. Every recovered message shows in your dashboard as an exact count, not an estimate.

Preserve Your "Replies in Minutes" Badge

Google says responsiveness affects LSA ranking. We verify every reply through the LSA API so no message lead goes unanswered.

Why SMS-Only AI Agents Miss Replies

A generic AI agent bolted onto LSA replies over SMS and trusts the "delivered" receipt. It has no view inside the LSA thread, so it never knows the reply vanished. Catching these takes API-level delivery verification, which only exists in a system built for LSA from the ground up. Ours backs you up even if you answer messages yourself.

Google Local Services Ads FAQ

What is LSA Command?

LSA Command is Google Local Services Ads software for marketing agencies. For every client it answers LSA calls and messages with AI, writes notes into every lead, texts and emails the client the moment a lead comes in, fills out Google's lead survey, grades every call, flags bad leads for credit, manages the service area, and gets more reviews.

How do I rank higher in Google Local Services Ads?

Google ranks Local Services Ads in an auction that weighs your bid, how likely your ad is to get a lead (including your responsiveness to calls and messages), and profile quality: rating, number of reviews, response time, photos, and verification checks. Responsiveness is usually the fastest to improve: answer every call and message quickly. Learn more →

Does missing calls hurt my Google LSA ranking?

Yes. Google says missed calls can hurt your responsiveness, which is a ranking factor. LSA Command rings your team first and has AI answer at 15 seconds, so every call is answered inside 30 seconds. Learn more →

How do I dispute a Google LSA lead?

Google now handles most LSA lead disputes through an automated credit system and asks advertisers to rate leads with lead feedback. LSA Command scores every lead, rates it back to Google, flags spam and invalid leads for credit, and tracks the credits Google records. Learn more →

What happens to Local Services Ads with Performance Max?

Google is moving LSA into Google Ads as a Performance Max campaign type. You still pay per lead and still show on Search and Maps, but bidding, budgets, and missed-call billing are changing. Learn more →

Who can buy LSA Command?

LSA Command works only with marketing agencies. Agencies run it for their home-service clients, including HVAC companies, plumbers, roofers, electricians and pool contractors. We do not sell directly to businesses. If you own a business, ask your marketing agency about LSA Command.
